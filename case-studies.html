Nordhaus Retail · Fashion

62% fewer stockouts in eight months.

Nordhaus was running demand forecasting on a weekly batch cycle — by the time the signal reached store replenishment, it was already stale. Fast-moving categories like outerwear and footwear had chronic stockout rates above 18%.

After deploying Veltrix's demand sensing module, Nordhaus moved to near-real-time replenishment triggers. The system detects a demand acceleration in a product within hours, not days, and automatically updates replenishment recommendations across all DCs.
