Our story

Built by operators, for operators.

We started Veltrix after seeing first-hand how broken supply chain visibility was — even at some of the world's largest companies. That had to change.

Founded 2019

Clarity where there used to be chaos.

Veltrix began with a simple observation: enterprise supply chains generate enormous amounts of data, but most of that data is trapped in disconnected systems that don't talk to each other.

Our founders — veterans of McKinsey's operations practice and Amazon's supply chain engineering team — set out to build the platform they wished had existed when they were in the field. A system that unifies all your supply chain data, applies machine learning to surface what matters, and delivers insight to the people who can act on it.

Today, Veltrix is trusted by 340+ enterprise customers across 28 countries, from mid-market manufacturers to Fortune 500 retailers. We are headquartered in our main office with teams across multiple global locations.

What we believe

Four principles. No compromises.

◈
Data without delay
Supply chain intelligence is only useful if it's in real time. We obsess over latency and data freshness — because a 48-hour-old alert is just a post-mortem.
◉
Integration over replacement
Your ERP took years to implement. We connect to it, not replace it. Veltrix is the intelligence layer that sits above your existing systems.
◌
Outcomes, not features
We measure our success in your KPIs — stockout rate, inventory turns, on-time delivery. A feature that doesn't improve an outcome doesn't ship.
◍
Security by default
SOC 2 Type II certified. GDPR and CCPA compliant. EU data residency available on all plans. Your supply chain data is the crown jewels — we protect it like it.
